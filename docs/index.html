How it works

Three steps. No account signup.

Copy

Log in on a site, open Session Copy, and copy the encrypted session from the active tab.

Share

Send the token or file through your usual channel — treat it like a password.

Paste

On another browser, paste or import. The extension opens the site and restores your session.

Privacy first. No accounts, no analytics, no cloud session database. Encrypted data stays on your device until you choose to share it.

Install

Available on the Chrome Web Store for Chrome and Chromium-based browsers.

Add to Chrome Build from source
